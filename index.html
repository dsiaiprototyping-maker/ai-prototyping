About This Session

This is a hands-on AI prototyping workshop exploring what teams can build using Claude as a coding collaborator. In 50 minutes, three teams will each add a real feature to this very page — live, in front of you.

The goal is not a polished product. The goal is understanding how the quality of your prompt shapes the quality of your output.
