Down the Rabbit Hole

The day opened with a welcome talk from Graham that set the tone perfectly — ambitious, honest, and genuinely exciting about where DS&I is heading. It was the kind of opener that makes you glad you showed up.

The morning's centrepiece was an interactive showcase of team projects. Sovereign AI made the case that owning your AI stack is a strategic advantage, not just a compliance checkbox — the demo was genuinely jaw-dropping. Knowledge Graphs showed how connecting data intelligently can unlock a level of search and reasoning that embeddings alone can't match. Each project was a reminder of the depth of talent sitting across DS&I.

The DS&I Engage session brought everyone into the conversation — a rare chance to hear from colleagues across the full breadth of the division and to think collectively about the direction we're heading. Candid, energising, and long overdue.

After a well-earned lunch, the closing talks wrapped up the themes of the day with a forward-looking lens. Then came the scavenger hunt — competitive, chaotic, and exactly what you want after a day of big thinking. No further comment on who won.
