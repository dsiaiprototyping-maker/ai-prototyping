Down the Rabbit Hole

The DSI AwayDay was everything we hoped it would be and more — a packed day of inspiration, experimentation, and honest conversation about where AI is taking us. The energy in the room was electric from the moment the first session kicked off.

We were treated to deep dives into two standout projects that left the whole team buzzing. Sovereign AI challenged everything we assumed about cloud dependency, making the case — compellingly — that owning your AI stack isn't just a security preference, it's a strategic advantage. The demo alone was worth the trip.

Then came NW AgentKit, a homegrown framework for building multi-step AI agents that actually work in the real world of enterprise constraints. Watching it orchestrate complex workflows in real time felt like a glimpse of how we'll all be working in two years. The team behind it deserves enormous credit for turning a bold idea into something you can ship.

Between sessions, the hallway conversations were just as rich — the kind of cross-team exchanges that only happen when you get everyone in the same room. We left with notebooks full of ideas and a genuine sense that DS&I is building something special.
